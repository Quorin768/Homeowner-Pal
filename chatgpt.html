How a turnover request actually runs

Ask for it in one sentence and ChatGPT does the work in steps you approve: it reads the guest's checkout time from HomeownerPal, schedules a turnover clean at the time you asked for (HomeownerPal attaches its turnover checklist automatically), then emails your preferred cleaner a no-login link to that branded checklist. The cleaning and the email are two separate confirmed actions, and the email is only reported as sent once the mail service accepts it.

How it works

HomeownerPal remembers. ChatGPT helps you use it.

HomeownerPal remains the home for your property records. ChatGPT doesn't replace that system — it gives you another way to work with it. Connect ChatGPT

Takes a few minutes

Use a desktop browser — that's what this setup is designed for. You'll need a ChatGPT plan that supports custom connectors.

Your HomeownerPal Server URL

Copy this — you'll paste it into ChatGPT in step 6:

https://mcp.homeownerpal.com/mcp Server URL

You'll sign into HomeownerPal and approve the connection — ChatGPT never sees your password.

Step by step, in ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT on the web — desktop browser recommended.

  2. Go to Settings → Plugins.

  3. Open Advanced Settings.

  4. Enable Developer Mode. ChatGPT shows a warning — that's expected for every custom connector.

  5. Return to Settings → Plugins and choose Create Plugin.

  6. Configure the plugin:

    Name: HomeownerPal
    Description: Access your HomeownerPal account and home/property information.
    Connection: Server URL
    Server URL: paste the URL copied above
    Authentication: OAuth

  7. Accept the Developer Mode warning and finish creating the plugin.

  8. Sign in to HomeownerPal in the popup and approve the connection.

  9. Start a new ChatGPT conversation and try it. Property managers: “Can you list all my managed properties in HomeownerPal?” Homeowners: “List my homes in HomeownerPal.”

Why Developer Mode?

HomeownerPal is currently awaiting completion of OpenAI's review process to become an officially published ChatGPT integration. Until that process is complete, connecting HomeownerPal requires ChatGPT's Developer Mode. Once HomeownerPal is publicly available through ChatGPT, we'll update these instructions with the simpler connection process.

The warning ChatGPT shows appears for every custom connector — it isn't specific to HomeownerPal. Only connect servers you trust; here you're connecting directly to HomeownerPal's own server.

Records, reports and hand-offs

Home + Portfolio

The paperwork side of owning — organized, searchable, and shareable.

  • Search documents by words: "find my roof warranty", "where is my furnace receipt"
  • File a receipt, invoice or policy from a photo or PDF into the right place
  • Track your home inventory of valuables for insurance and police reports

You're still in control

  • When an action would change your HomeownerPal records or communicate with someone on your behalf, ChatGPT confirms the action with you first. Looking things up needs no confirmation; creating, updating, logging, emailing, calling or archiving does.
  • A few actions are deliberately two-step: HomeownerPal reads the details back to you — the exact property, the cleaner's email address, the company and amount on an estimate — and only records the change on the second, confirmed call. Snoozing a reminder is the one small exception, since it changes nothing but a date you asked to move.
  • Nothing can be permanently deleted through ChatGPT. Reminders, policies and schedules are archived, keeping their history intact.

Privacy & permissions

  • ChatGPT only ever sees data tied to your signed-in HomeownerPal account. Row-level security enforces this on every request.
  • Look-up actions cannot change anything. Actions that write are flagged in the protocol, which is what prompts ChatGPT to ask you to confirm before running them. Archiving is flagged more strongly still.
  • There is no permanent-delete action. Records are archived rather than removed, so nothing is silently lost.
  • You approve the connection once via the HomeownerPal consent screen, and can revoke access at any time from your ChatGPT connector settings.
  • HomeownerPal never sends ChatGPT your password. It uses OAuth 2.1 with short-lived tokens.
  • Your conversations happen inside ChatGPT — read OpenAI's privacy policy for how chat history is stored.
FAQ

FAQ & troubleshooting

Do I need a paid ChatGPT plan?

Yes — you'll need a ChatGPT plan that supports custom connectors (accessed through Developer Mode), plus an active HomeownerPal membership, home membership, Realtor Pack, or Property Management plan.

Do I have to use a desktop browser?

For the setup itself, yes in practice — Developer Mode and Create Plugin live in ChatGPT's web settings and are far easier to complete on a desktop browser. Once connected, you can use HomeownerPal in ChatGPT wherever you normally chat.

The connection asks me to sign in repeatedly.

HomeownerPal uses OAuth 2.1 with short-lived tokens, so you may occasionally be asked to sign back in as a token expires. Approve the prompt from the HomeownerPal consent screen and you'll be reconnected.

Can ChatGPT change or delete my data?

ChatGPT can create, update, log or archive information in your HomeownerPal account, but only after it reads the action back to you and you confirm it. There is no permanent-delete action — records are archived, not removed, so nothing is silently lost.

Can it really schedule a turnaround clean after checkout?

Yes. See "How a turnover request actually runs" above — ChatGPT reads the guest's checkout time, schedules the clean with HomeownerPal's turnover checklist attached, and emails your preferred cleaner a no-login link, as two separate confirmed actions.

I connected it but ChatGPT says it can't see my data.

Check that you signed in with the same HomeownerPal account that holds your homes or properties — ChatGPT only ever sees data tied to the account you connected with. If it still can't find your records, disconnect and reconnect the plugin.

How do I disconnect?

You can revoke access at any time from your ChatGPT connector settings. HomeownerPal itself is unaffected — your records stay exactly as they are.

Install the HOP app

HOP works great as an app on your phone — quick to open the next time something goes sideways at home. It only takes a few taps.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top corner. Done — HOP is on your home screen.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the menu in the top-right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
