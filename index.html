My Home

Everything about your home. Ready when you need it.

When the AC quits, a storm hits, an insurance claim needs proof, or it's time to sell, the details about your home shouldn't be buried in drawers, old emails, camera rolls, or memory. My Home keeps them organized and ready when you need them.

The worst time to go looking for a serial number, a receipt or an appraisal is after something has already gone wrong.

My Home is your house's private record, built once, useful for as long as you own it.

When something breaks

Share equipment with your tech

Brand, model, serial, install date, photos and past service, send the whole equipment record to a contractor or technician in one tap, so they show up knowing what they're working on.

Before a loss

Document what you own

Build a home inventory of valuables with serials, values, receipts, appraisals and photos, and keep your insurance details alongside it. If there's a fire, storm, theft or water damage, you can show what you owned and what was in the home, and email a record straight to your adjuster or the police.

Year after year

Keep the home's history

Every service visit, repair, filter change, invoice, company and cost stays on the record, with reminders for what's coming up next. The history follows the house, not your memory. Selling the house? Transfer the home record to the next owner so its history goes with it.

What's inside

Your equipment record

HVAC, electrical panels, water heaters, appliances, brand, model, serial, install date and warranty, with manuals and maintenance parts.

Documents in one place

Receipts, invoices, warranties, manuals, inspections, permits and insurance paperwork, filed where you'll actually find them.

Photos that matter

Serial stickers, data plates, shutoff valves, breaker panels, damage and before/after shots, attached to the right item.

Trusted pros, ready when it matters

Save the plumber, electrician and HVAC tech you trust, handy for repairs, emergencies, insurance documentation, or selling and transferring the home.

Stop hunting. Start building your home record.

Add your equipment, your paperwork and your valuables once, then have it ready for the repair, the claim, the tech at the door, or the day you hand the keys to someone else.

Important, please read

HomeownerPal (HOP) is built and maintained by people with decades of combined hands-on trade experience. The guidance here is grounded in real field experience and is intended to help homeowners understand what's likely happening, what a fair price range looks like, and what questions to ask. That said, home systems are complex and every situation is different. Some issues simply require a qualified technician on-site to physically test, locate, and confirm the actual cause before any repair should be made.

Electricity, gas, and water systems can cause serious injury or death. If you smell burning, see smoke or sparks, hear sustained buzzing, or otherwise suspect an emergency, stop, leave the area, and call 911 and/or a licensed professional immediately. Do not attempt repairs you are not trained or permitted to perform; many jurisdictions legally require licensed and inspected work.

Install the HOP app

HOP works great as an app on your phone — quick to open the next time something goes sideways at home. It only takes a few taps.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top corner. Done — HOP is on your home screen.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the menu in the top-right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Confirm with Add / Install. Look for the bunny on your home screen.

HOP is always free, and the app is just our website saved to your home screen — no app store needed.
