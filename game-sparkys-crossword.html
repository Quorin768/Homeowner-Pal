      Install the HOP app

      HOP works great as an app on your phone — quick to open the next time something goes sideways at home. It only takes a few taps.

      On iPhone / iPad (Safari)

      1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
      2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
      3. Tap Add in the top corner. Done — HOP is on your home screen.

      On Android (Chrome)

      1. Tap the menu in the top-right of Chrome.
      2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
      3. Confirm with Add / Install. Look for the bunny on your home screen.

      HOP is always free, and the app is just our website saved to your home screen — no app store needed.
